# Aloe Vera: A Practical, Symbolic Gift Plant

Aloe vera offers something most gift plants don't — genuine practical household use, alongside a low-maintenance, succulent-like care profile.

## The Practical Appeal

Aloe vera gel has long been used topically for minor skin soothing, giving the plant a functional purpose beyond decoration — a recipient can actually use it, not just display it, which sets it apart from most other common gift plants.

## Care Requirements

Like other succulents, aloe vera stores water in its leaves and tolerates infrequent watering well, making it a reasonably forgiving choice for a recipient without much plant experience.

## A Caution Worth Noting

Aloe vera is toxic to cats and dogs if ingested, causing gastrointestinal upset — worth keeping in mind for a household with pets, similar to the caution that applies to several other common gift plants.
