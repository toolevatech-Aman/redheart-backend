# Plants for Welcoming a New Colleague

A modest plant is one of the most common, appropriately scaled welcome gestures for a new team member — practical, lasting, and not overly personal for a professional relationship still forming.

## Why This Suits a First-Day Welcome

Unlike flowers, which fade within a couple of weeks, a plant sits on the new colleague's desk for months, becoming a small, ongoing part of their workspace rather than a one-time gesture that disappears.

## Best Options for This Occasion

Snake plant and syngonium both tolerate inconsistent care and lower office light, making them well suited to a desk that may not get ideal conditions, and neither requires much attention from someone still learning their new role.

## Scaling for Seniority

For a more senior new hire, a slightly more premium plant like jade or a modest bonsai can suit a more formal welcome than the standard snake plant default.

## Quick Answers

**What's a good plant to welcome a new employee?**
Snake plant or syngonium — both low-maintenance and well suited to typical office lighting conditions.

**Should a more senior hire get a different plant?**
A slightly more premium option like jade or a modest bonsai can suit a more formal welcome for a senior new hire.
