# Plants for Graduation

For a graduate transitioning directly into a new job or field, a plant for their future workspace extends the celebration beyond the ceremony itself into whatever comes next.

## Why This Occasion Suits a Plant Gift

A graduation represents years of sustained effort, and a plant's own symbolism of patience and growth mirrors that journey more directly than a purely celebratory gift.

## Choosing Based on What Comes Next

For a graduate moving into a first job, a desk-appropriate plant like snake plant or jade suits their new workspace well. For one continuing into further study, a more modest, easy-care option suits a dorm or shared housing setting better.

## Pairing With Flowers or a Cake

A plant paired with sunflowers or a celebratory cake covers both the immediate achievement-signaling gesture and a lasting reminder of the milestone.

## Quick Answers

**Is a plant an appropriate graduation gift?**
Yes, particularly for a graduate moving into a new job, where a desk plant extends the celebration into their next chapter.

**What plant suits a graduate moving into a dorm or shared housing?**
A modest, easy-care option like snake plant or pothos suits a smaller, shared living space well.
