# Best Plants for a Home Office

A home office benefits from a different plant choice than a living room — something that creates a visual focal point without demanding much maintenance from someone focused on work.

## Why Bonsai and Jade Suit This Space Well

A home office is perfect for a bonsai or jade plant that creates a visual focal point without demanding much maintenance — both offer structured, deliberate visual presence suited to a desk or shelf.

## Considering Desk Space Specifically

For a desk itself rather than the broader office space, a smaller jade or a compact snake plant fits without crowding a work surface, while a bonsai often suits a shelf or side table better given its slightly larger footprint.

## What to Avoid

Larger, sprawling plants can create clutter or maintenance demands mismatched to a workspace meant to stay tidy and low-distraction.

## Quick Answers

**What plant is best for a home office desk?**
A compact jade or snake plant fits desk space well without demanding much maintenance.

**Is bonsai a good home office gift?**
Yes, particularly for a shelf or side table, where it creates a deliberate visual focal point.
