# Peace Lily: Why It's the Go-To Recovery Gift

Peace lily's popularity for get well soon gifting isn't accidental — its specific qualities suit recovery contexts better than most alternatives.

## What Makes It Fit This Occasion Specifically

Its white blooms convey calm and hope without tipping into anything somber, and it's low-fragrance, avoiding the discomfort a heavily scented arrangement can cause in an enclosed sickroom. It also helps purify indoor air, a genuine benefit for someone spending extended time in one room.

## Beyond Get Well Soon: Where Else Peace Lily Fits

Paired with pink carnations, it makes one of the most complete Mother's Day gifts available — the carnations for immediate warmth, the peace lily for a lasting, low-maintenance presence in her home.

## Care Basics

Peace lily is more sensitive to drying out than snake plant or jade, but it visibly droops as a clear signal it needs water — making it easier to read than plants that show distress only after real damage.

## Quick Answers

**Why is peace lily specifically chosen for recovery gifts?**
Its calm white blooms, low fragrance, and air-purifying qualities suit a sickroom better than more vibrant or heavily scented alternatives.

**How do I know when to water a peace lily?**
It will visibly droop, a reliable signal that's easier to read than plants that show no early warning of distress.
