# Plants for a Housewarming: Money Plant vs Jade

Both plants carry prosperity symbolism, but subtle differences in growth habit and presentation make one or the other a better fit depending on the new home's specific space.

## Why Both Are Traditional Choices

Money plant and jade are the most popular housewarming plants, both carrying prosperity associations especially meaningful in the context of a new home — an implicit wish for good fortune to grow alongside the household.

## Choosing Based on Display Style

Money plant's trailing growth suits shelves or hanging displays and offers flexibility (including growing in water alone), while jade's structured, succulent-like form suits a defined tabletop or desk placement better.

## Considering the New Homeowner's Experience

Both are relatively low-maintenance, which matters for a recipient likely too occupied with moving logistics to manage a demanding plant in the first few weeks.

## Quick Answers

**Should I choose money plant or jade for a housewarming?**
Either works well — money plant suits flexible, trailing display, while jade suits a more structured, defined placement.

**Are both low-maintenance enough for a busy new homeowner?**
Yes, both are relatively forgiving, suited to someone occupied with the demands of moving in.
