# ZZ Plant: The Ultimate Neglect-Proof Gift

Among low-maintenance gift plants, ZZ plant stands out for tolerating an unusually wide range of neglect — low light, infrequent watering, and inconsistent care — while still maintaining a glossy, attractive appearance.

## Why It Suits Even the Most Inconsistent Plant Owner

ZZ plant's thick, waxy leaves store water efficiently, allowing it to go longer between waterings than most common gift plants, including snake plant, making it a strong option for a recipient with a genuinely poor track record of plant care.

## Visual Appeal Without High Maintenance

Its glossy, dark green leaves offer a more polished, deliberate appearance than some other low-maintenance options, making it a strong choice for a recipient who wants their space to look considered without much ongoing effort.

## A Caution Worth Noting

ZZ plant contains calcium oxalate crystals and should be kept out of reach of pets and small children who might chew on it, similar to several other common houseplants — worth mentioning if the recipient's household includes either.

## Quick Answers

**How does ZZ plant compare to snake plant for low maintenance?**
ZZ plant can tolerate slightly longer gaps between waterings, thanks to its water-storing leaves, making it comparably or even more forgiving than snake plant.

**Is ZZ plant safe around pets?**
No — like several common houseplants, it contains calcium oxalate crystals and should be kept away from pets or small children who might chew on it.
