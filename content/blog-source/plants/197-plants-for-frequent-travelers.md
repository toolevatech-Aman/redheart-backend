# Choosing Plants for Someone Who Travels Often

A frequent traveler needs a plant that can tolerate extended gaps in care, not just a generally low-maintenance option — a meaningful distinction worth considering before choosing.

## Why Standard Low-Maintenance Isn't Always Enough

Many "low-maintenance" plants still need water on a roughly weekly basis, which doesn't necessarily suit someone gone for two or three weeks at a stretch — a more specifically drought-tolerant option matters more here than general low-maintenance framing suggests.

## Best Options for Extended Absences

Succulents and cacti, which store water in their leaves or stems, tolerate longer gaps between watering than most foliage plants, including snake plant, making them a stronger fit for a genuinely frequent traveler.

## Snake Plant as a Middle-Ground Option

Snake plant remains a reasonable choice for shorter trips, tolerating a week or two without water reasonably well, even if succulents handle longer absences with more ease.

## Quick Answers

**What's the best plant for someone who travels frequently?**
Succulents or cacti, given their ability to store water and tolerate longer gaps between waterings than typical foliage plants.

**Is snake plant good enough for a frequent traveler?**
It works reasonably well for shorter trips, but succulents handle longer absences with more ease.
