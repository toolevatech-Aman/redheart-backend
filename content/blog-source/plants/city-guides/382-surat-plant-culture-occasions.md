# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Surat

Surat's identity as a major diamond and textile trading hub gives the city's plant-gifting culture a distinctly commercial dimension alongside Gujarat's broader prosperity-focused festival traditions.

## Business Milestones and Prosperity Symbolism

Given Surat's prominence in diamond cutting and textile manufacturing, bonsai and jade have found genuine relevance as gifts marking business success and trade milestones, carrying meaningful symbolism beyond a purely decorative gesture.

## Navratri and Diwali's Prosperity Themes

Consistent with Gujarat's broader festival traditions, money plant and jade suit Surat's Navratri and Diwali gifting well, particularly given the region's strong cultural association between prosperity and celebration.

## Housewarmings in a Growing City

Given Surat's ongoing residential growth tied to its business success, housewarming gifting runs at real volume, with money plant and jade functioning as standard prosperity-symbolic accompaniments to flowers.
