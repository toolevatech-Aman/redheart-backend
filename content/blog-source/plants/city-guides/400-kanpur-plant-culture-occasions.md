# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Kanpur

Kanpur's identity as the leather and textile capital of Uttar Pradesh, alongside its status as home to IIT Kanpur, gives the city a plant-gifting culture shaped by both industrial business and academic celebration.

## Business Milestones and Corporate Gifting
Kanpur's manufacturing heritage has built a business community where bonsai and jade suit trade-relationship and commercial-milestone gifting.

## IIT Kanpur Events
The presence of IIT Kanpur creates a genuine plant-gifting category tied to academic achievements, with desk plants suiting graduation and campus celebrations.

## Housewarmings
Money plant and jade suit Kanpur's housewarming culture, carrying standard prosperity symbolism.
