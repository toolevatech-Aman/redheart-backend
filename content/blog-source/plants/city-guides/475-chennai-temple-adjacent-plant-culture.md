# Chennai's Temple-Adjacent Plant Culture

Given Chennai's deep-rooted temple and devotional flower culture, plant gifting has found a complementary role around personal and modern occasions rather than the city's core religious practices.

## Traditional Occasions Keep Their Own Focus
Major festivals like Pongal center on traditional flower and food offerings, with plants more relevant to accompanying family celebrations than the core traditions themselves.

## A Growing Corporate and Housewarming Culture
Chennai's expanding IT and manufacturing sectors, alongside steady residential growth, support genuine demand for desk plants and housewarming gifts like money plant and jade.
