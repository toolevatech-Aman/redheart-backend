# Madurai's Temple Plant Traditions

Given Madurai's deep temple-centered identity, plant gifting here has found its place around personal and modern occasions rather than the city's core devotional practices, which remain rooted in traditional flower offerings.

## Traditional Occasions Keep Their Own Focus
Major temple festivals like the Chithirai Festival center on traditional flower offerings, with plants more relevant to accompanying family celebrations than temple rituals themselves.

## Housewarmings and Modern Gifting
Money plant and jade suit Madurai's housewarming culture, carrying prosperity symbolism that sits comfortably alongside the city's traditional flower and sweets gifting.

## What This Means for Buyers
For temple-related occasions in Madurai, a plant works best as a complementary gesture rather than the centerpiece; for housewarmings and personal gifting, standard prosperity plants suit well.
