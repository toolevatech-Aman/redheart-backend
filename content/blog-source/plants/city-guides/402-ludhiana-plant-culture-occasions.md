# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Ludhiana

Ludhiana's identity as the hosiery and textile capital of Asia, alongside Punjab's generous celebratory culture, shapes a plant-gifting culture favoring substantial, well-presented choices.

## Business Success and Prosperity Symbolism
Given Ludhiana's manufacturing prominence, bonsai and jade suit gifting tied to business milestones and trade relationships.

## Housewarmings With Punjab's Generous Character
Money plant and jade suit Ludhiana's housewarming culture, reflecting the region's broader reputation for warm, abundant hospitality.

## Lohri and Baisakhi
Plants complement family celebrations during these major Punjab festivals, alongside traditional sweets and gestures.
