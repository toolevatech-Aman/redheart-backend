Vadodara's identity as a former princely capital, shaped by the Gaekwad dynasty and Laxmi Vilas Palace, gives plant gifting here a particular elegance reflecting the city's royal heritage.

The Gaekwad legacy gives Vadodara a cultural association with refinement that extends into plant gifting — bonsai in particular suits the city's own aesthetic sensibility for considered, well-presented gestures.

## Navratri's UNESCO Recognition

Given Vadodara's UNESCO-recognized Navratri Garba tradition, plants have found a complementary role in extended family celebrations throughout the festival's nine nights.

## Housewarmings and Corporate Gifting

Money plant and jade suit housewarming gifting, while bonsai suits senior or executive-level corporate gifting given the city's business growth alongside its heritage identity.
