# Bangalore's Garden City Plant Legacy

Bangalore's long-standing reputation as the Garden City, reflected in spaces like Lalbagh and Cubbon Park, gives the city a genuine cultural relationship with plants that extends into gifting expectations.

## A City Where Greenery Is Civic Identity
Given Bangalore's historic green spaces, the population has a stronger baseline familiarity with plant variety than in many other Indian cities, shaping expectations for thoughtful, well-chosen plant gifts.

## Balancing Heritage With Modern Tech-Hub Growth
Even as Bangalore has grown into a major tech hub, this garden-city identity persists, supporting demand for both traditional prosperity plants and design-forward options favored by younger residents.

## Quick Answers

**Why does Bangalore have a stronger plant-gifting culture than average?**
Its historic identity as the Garden City, reflected in spaces like Lalbagh, has cultivated genuine local familiarity and appreciation for plants.

**Has Bangalore's tech growth changed this relationship with plants?**
Not fundamentally — the garden-city identity persists alongside modern growth, supporting both traditional and design-forward plant gifting.
