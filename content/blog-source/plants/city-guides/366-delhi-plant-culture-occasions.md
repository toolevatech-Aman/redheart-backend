# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Delhi

Delhi's mix of traditional festival observance and modern gifting habits has made plants a genuinely growing category here, particularly for occasions where a lasting gesture matters more than a fleeting one.

Given how much of Delhi is perpetually expanding — from Dwarka to Rohini to the newer developments along the city's edges — housewarming gifting runs at real volume here, and money plant or jade has become a standard accompaniment to flowers for exactly this occasion.

## Corporate Gifting in a City Full of Offices

Delhi's dense concentration of corporate offices and government institutions makes desk plants a genuine fixture of office culture — snake plants and syngonium in particular suit the city's substantial workplace gifting habits.

## Diwali's Prosperity Symbolism

Given Diwali's association with prosperity, money plant and jade have found real significance in Delhi's festival gifting, distinguishing a gift from the standard dry fruit box that remains the more traditional default.
