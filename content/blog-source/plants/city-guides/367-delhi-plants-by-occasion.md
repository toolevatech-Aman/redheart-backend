# Best Plants for Every Occasion in Delhi

Delhi's occasion calendar spans housewarmings, corporate gifting, festivals, and milestone celebrations — matching plant choice to each occasion helps it land as intentionally as flowers or cake would.

## Housewarmings

Money plant or jade suits Delhi's active housewarming gifting well, given how frequently the city's residential developments are expanding, carrying prosperity symbolism especially meaningful for a new home.

## Diwali

Money plant and jade suit Diwali's prosperity associations, distinguishing a gift from the standard dry fruit box that remains Delhi's more traditional festival default.

## Corporate and Office Gifting

Snake plants and syngonium suit Delhi's substantial office culture, offering practical, low-maintenance desk plants for new colleagues or client gifting.

## Weddings and Milestone Anniversaries

Bonsai paired with flowers suits milestone gifting tied to Delhi's significant wedding season and significant anniversary celebrations.

## Get Well Soon

Peace lily suits recovery gifting in Delhi, offering calm, low-fragrance presence well suited to a longer recovery period.

## Retirement and Farewell

Bonsai suits retirement gifting for long-tenured colleagues, given Delhi's substantial corporate and government workforce.
