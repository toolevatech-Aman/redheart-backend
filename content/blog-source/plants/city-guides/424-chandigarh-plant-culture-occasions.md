Chandigarh's identity as India's first planned city, designed by Le Corbusier, gives it a built-in relationship with greenery that extends naturally into plant gifting.

Chandigarh's deliberate urban planning incorporated extensive gardens, giving the city a genuine civic relationship with greenery that shapes local appreciation for plant gifts.

Money plant and jade suit Chandigarh's housewarming culture, reflecting the shared Punjab-Haryana reputation for generous, well-presented celebration.

## Lohri and Baisakhi
Plants complement family celebrations during these major festivals, shared between the city's Punjab and Haryana traditions.
