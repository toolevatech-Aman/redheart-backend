# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Kolkata

Kolkata's identity as a cultural and artistic capital extends into a genuine appreciation for plants as thoughtful, considered gifts, particularly given the city's strong sense of aesthetic presentation.

## A City That Values Craft and Presentation

Kolkata's strong artistic and cultural identity, visible in its approach to Durga Puja and its literary heritage, extends into an appreciation for well-chosen, presentation-conscious gifts — a quality that shapes local plant-gifting expectations beyond what's typical elsewhere.

## Durga Puja and the Festival Season

Given how central Durga Puja is to Kolkata's identity, plants have found a role in the extended festival season, particularly as housewarming-adjacent gifts for families settling into new homes ahead of the celebration.

## Housewarmings in an Established City

Kolkata's established residential neighborhoods and steady population mean housewarming gifting remains a genuine occasion, with money plant and jade functioning as standard prosperity-symbolic gifts.

## Corporate Gifting in a Business Hub

As an important business center in Eastern India, Kolkata generates demand for desk plants — snake plants and syngonium suiting office culture, bonsai suiting senior or client-level gifting.

## What This Means for Choosing a Plant

Given Kolkata's genuine appreciation for craft and presentation, a well-chosen, thoughtfully presented plant tends to be noticed and valued here more than in cities where plant gifting is treated more casually.
