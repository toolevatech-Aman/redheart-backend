# Gurgaon's Corporate Plant Gifting Standard

Given how dominant office culture is in Gurgaon's daily life, plant gifting here has settled into a fairly predictable, professionally-driven pattern distinct from cities with deeper regional tradition.

## Standardized Desk Plant Gifting
Snake plants and syngonium have become close to a default welcome gift for new hires across Gurgaon's dense concentration of multinational offices, given their reliability under office lighting and minimal care needs.

## Executive Gifting Follows Its Own Convention
For senior client or leadership-level gifting specifically, bonsai has emerged as the standard premium choice, valued for its visible, daily presence on a desk or shelf.

## Quick Answers

**Why is snake plant such a common welcome gift in Gurgaon?**
Its reliability under typical office lighting and minimal care requirements suit the city's dense corporate office environment well.

**What plant suits senior or executive gifting in Gurgaon?**
Bonsai, valued for its visible, lasting presence and premium signaling.
