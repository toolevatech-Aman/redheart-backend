# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Amritsar

Amritsar's identity as home to the Golden Temple gives the city a devotional character alongside Punjab's generous celebratory culture, shaping how plant gifting fits into both registers.

Given the Golden Temple's centrality to Amritsar's identity, traditional devotional offerings remain core to religious occasions, with plants more relevant to accompanying family celebrations.

Respectfully presented plants suit Gurpurab gifting, while Baisakhi sees plants complementing warm family celebrations.
