# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Bhopal

Bhopal's identity as the City of Lakes, combined with its Nawabi heritage and role as Madhya Pradesh's capital, shapes a plant-gifting culture built on natural beauty and refined tradition.

## A City That Values Refinement and Natural Beauty
Bhopal's history under the Begums of Bhopal, alongside its lake-centered civic identity, gives the city an appreciation for thoughtful, well-presented gifts, including plants.

## Housewarmings and Government Gifting
Money plant and jade suit Bhopal's housewarming culture, while the city's role as Madhya Pradesh's capital generates plant demand tied to government functions.

## Navratri and Dussehra
Plants complement family celebrations during this extended festival period, similar to nearby Indore.
