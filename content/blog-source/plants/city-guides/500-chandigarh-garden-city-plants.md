# Chandigarh's Garden City Plant Sensibility

Chandigarh's deliberate urban design, built around extensive green space from its founding, gives the city a genuine civic relationship with plants that extends into gifting.

## A City Designed With Greenery Built In
Given Chandigarh's planned layout under Le Corbusier, residents have grown up with more deliberate, designed green space than most Indian cities offer, shaping an appreciation for thoughtfully chosen plants over generic options.

## Well-Composed Choices Suit the City
Given this design-conscious backdrop, structured plants like bonsai or jade tend to suit local aesthetic sensibility better than a casually chosen option.

## Quick Answers

**Why does Chandigarh have a strong civic relationship with plants?**
Its founding design under Le Corbusier deliberately incorporated extensive green space, shaping a population with more exposure to designed greenery than most cities.

**What plant suits Chandigarh's aesthetic sensibility?**
Structured, well-composed options like bonsai or jade, matching the city's design-conscious character.
