# Ahmedabad's Prosperity-Focused Plant Culture

Gujarat's strong cultural association between prosperity and celebration gives plant gifting in Ahmedabad a particularly meaningful symbolic weight, especially around business and festival occasions.

## Business Success and Plant Symbolism
Given Ahmedabad's strong trade and business culture, bonsai and jade have found genuine relevance as gifts marking commercial success, carrying meaning beyond a purely decorative gesture.

## Extended Festival Season Plant Gifting
Given the city's dense festival calendar spanning Navratri through Diwali and Bestu Varas, plants complement gifting across this extended window more than in cities with a single festival peak.
