# Surat's Trade-Driven Plant Gifting Culture

Surat's prominence in diamond cutting and textile manufacturing has built a plant-gifting culture where bonsai and jade carry genuine meaning as symbols of business achievement.

## Plants as Markers of Commercial Success
Given how central trade success is to Surat's civic identity, gifting a bonsai or jade plant to mark a business milestone carries more resonance here than in cities without the same commercial character.

## Housewarmings in a Growing City
Money plant and jade also suit Surat's active housewarming culture, given the city's ongoing residential growth tied to its business success.
