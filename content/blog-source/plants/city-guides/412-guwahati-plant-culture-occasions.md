# Why Plant Gifting Means So Much in Guwahati

Guwahati's role as the gateway to Northeast India, combined with its Kamakhya devotional significance, gives the city a plant-gifting culture shaped by distinctive Assamese tradition.

## Bihu and Modern Plant Gifting
Given Bihu's centrality to Guwahati's cultural identity, plants have increasingly found a place in the festival's celebrations, particularly among younger generations.

## Housewarmings
Money plant and jade suit Guwahati's housewarming culture, carrying standard prosperity symbolism.

## Ambubachi Mela and Devotional Occasions
Traditional offerings remain central to this pilgrimage event centered on the Kamakhya Temple, with plants more relevant to accompanying celebrations.

**Is plant gifting significant for Bihu in Guwahati?**
