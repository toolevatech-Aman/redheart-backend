# Kolkata's Presentation-Conscious Plant Culture

Kolkata's strong artistic and cultural identity extends into a genuine appreciation for thoughtfully presented plant gifts, distinct from cities where presentation matters less.

Given Kolkata's cultural association with Durga Puja's elaborate artistry and its literary heritage, a well-chosen, well-presented plant tends to be noticed and appreciated more here than a generic option.
