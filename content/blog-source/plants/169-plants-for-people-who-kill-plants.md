# Plants for People Who Kill Every Plant They Own

For a recipient with a self-admitted track record of plant failure, choosing genuinely forgiving options matters more than choosing something visually striking.

## The Most Forgiving Options Available

Money plant, snake plant, pothos, and syngonium are consistently recommended for first-time or historically unsuccessful plant owners — all tolerate inconsistent watering and a range of light conditions without much drama.

## Why Snake Plant Deserves Special Mention Here

Snake plant tolerates low light and irregular watering better than almost any other common gift plant, making it arguably the single safest choice for someone with a genuinely poor track record.

## What to Avoid for This Recipient

Bonsai and peace lily, while excellent plants, both require more consistent attention than a habitual plant-killer is likely to provide — better suited to a different recipient or a later gift once confidence has built up.

## Reframing the Gift as Low-Stakes

Presenting the plant as genuinely low-effort, rather than a challenge to finally "get plants right," can reduce the pressure a recipient with a history of failure might otherwise feel about the gift.

## Quick Answers

**What's the single most forgiving gift plant?**
Snake plant, given its tolerance for low light and irregular watering.

**Should I avoid demanding plants like bonsai for this recipient?**
Yes — bonsai and peace lily both need more consistent care than someone with a history of plant failure is likely to provide.
