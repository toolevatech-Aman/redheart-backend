# Plants for Anniversaries: Pairing With Roses

Anniversaries are an increasingly popular occasion for plant gifting, especially when combined with flowers rather than chosen as a standalone gift.

## The Two-Layered Gift Structure

A peace lily or jade plant alongside a bouquet of roses creates a two-layered gift — the flowers make the immediate romantic statement, the plant becomes the lasting reminder of the occasion.

## Milestone Anniversaries Specifically

For milestone anniversaries, a premium bonsai paired with roses is one of the most thoughtful combinations available — the bonsai's symbolism of patience and cultivated growth mirrors the years the relationship represents.

## Why This Combination Outperforms Flowers Alone

Flowers fade within one to two weeks; the plant continues, giving the anniversary a presence beyond the day itself in a way flowers alone can't provide.

## Quick Answers

**Why pair a plant with roses for an anniversary instead of flowers alone?**
The flowers deliver the immediate romantic statement, while the plant becomes a lasting reminder that continues well past the flowers' vase life.

**What plant suits a milestone anniversary specifically?**
A premium bonsai, given its symbolism of patience and cultivated growth over time.
