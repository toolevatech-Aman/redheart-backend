# Snake Plant vs Money Plant vs Jade: Which to Choose

These three dominate low-maintenance plant gifting in India, but they suit different spaces and symbolic purposes worth understanding before defaulting to whichever is most familiar.

## Snake Plant: Structural and Nearly Indestructible

Snake plant tolerates low light and irregular watering better than almost any other common gift plant, making it the safest choice for a recipient with no plant-care track record, or for a dim office corner where other plants would struggle.

## Money Plant: Flexible and Symbolically Rich

Money plant's prosperity association makes it the default choice for housewarmings, and its ability to grow even in water alone (not just soil) gives it more display flexibility than snake plant or jade.

## Jade: Structured and Prosperity-Coded

Jade shares money plant's prosperity symbolism but with a more structured, succulent-like form — better suited to a home office or desk where a defined visual focal point matters more than money plant's trailing growth habit.

## Choosing Based on Space and Symbolism

**Dim rooms or offices** → snake plant, given its low-light tolerance.
**Housewarmings, flexible display** → money plant.
**Desks, structured focal points** → jade.

## Quick Answers

**Which is hardest to kill?**
Snake plant, given its tolerance for low light and irregular watering.

**Which is best for a housewarming specifically?**
Money plant, given its strong, well-established prosperity symbolism for new homes.
