# Plants for a Friend

Since plants carry no romantic ambiguity, friend-directed plant gifting has the same freedom that governs friend-directed flowers — personality and practicality can guide the choice more than convention.

## Choosing Based on Their Track Record With Plants

For a friend known to struggle with plant care, low-maintenance options — snake plant, pothos — remove the risk of the gift becoming a source of guilt rather than enjoyment.

## Choosing Based on Their Space

A friend in a small apartment is well served by a compact syngonium or snake plant; one with a larger home or a dedicated plant collection might appreciate something more distinctive, like bonsai.

## Occasions That Suit a Friend's Plant Gift

Birthdays, housewarmings, congratulations, or an unprompted gesture all work well, following the same range of occasions appropriate for friend-directed gifting generally.

## Quick Answers

**What plant is safest to gift a friend who's not good with plants?**
Snake plant or pothos, both highly tolerant of inconsistent care.

**Should I consider a friend's living space before choosing a plant?**
Yes — a compact plant suits a small apartment, while a larger or more distinctive option suits a friend with more space or existing plant experience.
