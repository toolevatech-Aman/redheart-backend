# How to Choose a Plant Based on Someone's Gardening Experience

Matching plant choice to the recipient's actual comfort level with plant care avoids the common mismatch of gifting something too demanding for someone with no track record.

## First-Time Plant Owners

First-time plant owners should receive low-maintenance plants — money plant, snake plant, pothos, syngonium — all forgiving of inconsistent care and adaptable to a range of light conditions.

## Experienced Plant Owners

For someone with genuine gardening experience or an established plant collection, a more demanding option like bonsai can feel more personally chosen, since it matches an existing interest rather than introducing an unfamiliar responsibility.

## When You're Unsure of Their Experience Level

Defaulting to a low-maintenance option is generally the safer choice when experience level is unknown — an overly demanding plant risks becoming a source of guilt rather than enjoyment if it struggles under inexperienced care.

## Quick Answers

**What plant should I choose for someone who's never owned a plant?**
Money plant, snake plant, pothos, or syngonium — all low-maintenance and forgiving of inconsistent care.

**Is bonsai a good gift for a plant novice?**
Generally not the best first choice — it requires more consistent attention than most beginner-friendly options.
