# Plants for a Farewell

A plant fits a farewell's bittersweet mix of celebration and goodbye particularly well, since it represents something the departing colleague carries forward into their next chapter rather than something left behind with the role.

## Why Plants Suit This Occasion's Dual Nature

Unlike a cake, which is consumed and gone, a plant continues into whatever comes next for the recipient — a fitting symbol for a farewell that marks an ending alongside a new beginning.

## Scaling to the Relationship and Tenure

For a long-tenured colleague, a bonsai carries the same patience-and-cultivated-growth symbolism used in retirement gifting. For a shorter tenure or less established relationship, a more modest plant like snake plant or syngonium still carries some of that same "something that continues" symbolism.

## Team vs. Individual Farewell Gifts

A modest plant works well as a team-funded farewell gift, while a more personally chosen option suits a closer individual relationship as an addition to the group gesture.

## Quick Answers

**Why is a plant a fitting farewell gift?**
It represents something the departing colleague carries forward, rather than something left behind — fitting the occasion's mix of ending and new beginning.

**Should a farewell plant differ based on tenure?**
Yes — a bonsai suits a longer tenure's cultivated-growth symbolism, while a more modest plant suits a shorter or less established relationship.
