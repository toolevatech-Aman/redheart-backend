# Plants for Corporate Gifting

For senior clients or executives, a hamper with a bonsai plant, fine chocolates, and a curated flower arrangement signals premium quality and deliberate thought.

## Why Plants Suit Professional Relationships Well

A bonsai stays on a desk or shelf and is seen daily — a more lasting corporate impression than consumables alone, avoiding the romantic ambiguity flowers can sometimes carry in professional contexts.

## Choosing Based on Seniority and Occasion

**New colleagues** → a modest snake plant or syngonium, practical and not overly personal.
**Senior clients or executives** → bonsai, signaling premium quality and respect.
**Festival client gifting (Diwali)** → jade or money plant, tying prosperity symbolism to the business relationship.

## Why This Beats Purely Consumable Gifting

Unlike chocolates or cake, which are consumed and forgotten, a plant occupies the recipient's space and daily awareness — a distinction that matters more in professional relationships built on ongoing impressions.

**What plant suits a senior client gift?**
Bonsai, given its premium signaling and lasting daily presence on a desk or shelf.
