# Bulk Plant Gifting for Corporate Events

Distributing plants across an entire office or event attendee list involves different planning considerations than a single individual gift, similar to bulk cake ordering for large gatherings.

## Choosing a Uniform, Reliable Option for Scale

For a large distribution, a single low-maintenance, broadly appealing option — snake plant or syngonium — tends to work better than trying to personalize each recipient's plant individually, given the practical constraints of scale.

## Planning Lead Time for Bulk Orders

Larger plant orders generally benefit from more advance notice than a single gift, since bulk sourcing and preparation take more coordination than a standard individual order.

## Why Plants Suit Corporate Bulk Gifting Well

Unlike bulk flower or cake orders, which face freshness timing pressure, plants don't have the same narrow freshness window, giving more flexibility in exactly when a large batch is prepared relative to the event date.

A single low-maintenance, broadly appealing option like snake plant or syngonium tends to work best for large-scale distribution.
