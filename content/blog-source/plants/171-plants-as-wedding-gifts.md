# Plants as Wedding Gifts

For couples who've already established their homes and don't need traditional household gifts, a plant offers a symbolically fitting alternative or addition to flowers.

## The Symbolism Behind This Choice

A plant that grows alongside the couple in their new home carries a kind of symbolism — persistence, shared care, something that continues — that resonates with many recipients more than a bouquet that will fade within the week.

## Choosing a Plant for a Wedding Specifically

A more premium option, such as a bonsai or a substantial jade plant, suits the significance of a wedding gift better than a very basic low-maintenance plant, mirroring the same premium-choice logic that applies to milestone anniversary gifting.

## Pairing With Flowers or a Cake

A plant paired with white or soft-pastel wedding flowers creates a layered gift — the flowers deliver an immediate elegant statement, the plant becomes a lasting presence in the couple's new shared home.

## Quick Answers

**Is a plant an appropriate wedding gift?**
Yes, particularly for couples who already have established homes — it offers a symbolically fitting, lasting alternative to more traditional household gifts.

**What type of plant suits a wedding gift?**
A more premium option like bonsai or a substantial jade plant suits the occasion's significance better than a very basic low-maintenance choice.
