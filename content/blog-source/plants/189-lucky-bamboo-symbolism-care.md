# Lucky Bamboo: Symbolism and Care

Lucky bamboo carries a distinct prosperity and good fortune symbolism, closely associated with Feng Shui practice, offering a different cultural angle from money plant or jade's more general prosperity association.

## The Symbolism Behind Lucky Bamboo

Often gifted specifically for good fortune and positive energy, lucky bamboo's symbolism is tied closely to Feng Shui tradition, where the number of stalks in an arrangement can carry specific meaning — worth considering for a recipient who follows or appreciates this tradition specifically.

## Care Requirements

Lucky bamboo is commonly grown in water rather than soil, similar to how money plant can also be grown this way, making it a low-maintenance, visually distinctive option that doesn't require traditional potting.

## Choosing Lucky Bamboo vs. Money Plant or Jade

For a recipient with a specific interest in Feng Shui or a more design-forward aesthetic, lucky bamboo's distinctive look and specific symbolism can feel more personally chosen than the broader default of money plant or jade.
