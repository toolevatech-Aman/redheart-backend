# Plants for Get Well Soon

For a longer recovery, a plant offers something cut flowers can't — presence that continues well beyond the illness itself.

## Why Peace Lily Leads This Category

Its white blooms convey calm, care, and hope, and it's genuinely useful in a sickroom given its low fragrance and air-purifying qualities, exactly the message you want to send someone in recovery.

## Plant vs. Cut Flowers for This Occasion

A bouquet wilts around the same time the recipient is hopefully starting to feel better; a plant is still there weeks later, offering something to tend rather than something tied to the memory of being unwell.

## When to Choose a Plant Over Flowers

For a longer recovery, especially one continuing at home, a plant's staying power outperforms a bouquet's short vase life — reserve flowers for a shorter hospital stay where a longer-lasting gift isn't as necessary.

## Quick Answers

**Is a plant better than flowers for a long recovery?**
Generally yes — a plant continues well past the recovery period, while flowers fade within the timeframe of a typical illness.

**What plant is best for a sickroom specifically?**
Peace lily, given its low fragrance and calming, non-somber white blooms.
