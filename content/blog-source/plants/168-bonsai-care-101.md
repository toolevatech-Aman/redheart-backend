# Bonsai Care 101: A Beginner's Guide

Bonsai requires more consistent attention than snake plant, money plant, or jade — worth understanding before gifting one to someone without prior bonsai experience, or before caring for one yourself.

## Light Requirements

Most bonsai varieties need bright, consistent light, generally more demanding than the low-light tolerance of plants like snake plant — placement near a well-lit window matters more here than for most other common gift plants.

## Watering: More Frequent, More Attentive

Bonsai typically needs more frequent, lighter watering than succulent-adjacent plants like jade, since its shallow pot and compact root system dry out faster than a standard-sized pot would.

## Pruning: The Ongoing Commitment

Unlike most low-maintenance gift plants, bonsai benefits from occasional pruning to maintain its shape — a genuine ongoing commitment rather than a one-time care setup, which is part of what gives the plant its symbolic association with patience.

## Setting Expectations for a Gift Recipient

If gifting bonsai to someone without prior experience, including brief care guidance or setting realistic expectations about the attention it requires helps avoid the recipient feeling overwhelmed by a plant that's more demanding than the low-maintenance defaults they might expect.

## Quick Answers

**Is bonsai harder to care for than other common gift plants?**
Yes, generally — it needs more light, more frequent watering, and occasional pruning compared to plants like snake plant or jade.

**Should I warn a gift recipient about bonsai's care needs?**
It's a good idea, particularly if they have no prior bonsai experience, so they aren't caught off guard by the attention it requires.
