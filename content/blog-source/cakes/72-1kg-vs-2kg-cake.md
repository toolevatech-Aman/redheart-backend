# 1kg vs 2kg Cake: Which Should You Order for a Party?

This is one of the most common practical decisions in cake ordering, and getting it right mostly comes down to an honest guest count rather than guesswork.

## The Basic Serving Math

A one-kilogram cake generally serves 8 to 10 people, while a two-kilogram cake serves roughly double that — around 15 to 20 people. The relationship isn't perfectly linear since larger cakes can sometimes yield slightly more efficient slicing, but this rule of thumb holds well enough for most planning purposes.

## When 1kg Is Enough

A one-kilogram cake suits a smaller gathering — close family, a handful of friends, or an intimate celebration where 8 to 10 servings comfortably covers the group. It's also a reasonable choice even for a slightly larger gathering if the cake is one part of a broader spread with other desserts.

## When 2kg Makes More Sense

A two-kilogram cake suits a larger birthday party, a bigger family gathering, or an office celebration involving a full team. It also makes more visual impact as a centerpiece, given its larger size — worth considering if presentation matters as much as serving capacity.

## What If the Guest Count Falls in Between?

If your gathering sits between the two sizes — say, 12 people — it's generally safer to size up to 2kg rather than order exactly to the minimum, since running short is a more noticeable shortfall at a celebration than having a modest surplus.

## Quick Answers

**How many people does a 1kg cake serve compared to 2kg?**
A 1kg cake generally serves 8 to 10 people, while a 2kg cake serves roughly 15 to 20 — close to double, though not perfectly linear.

**Should I round up or down if my guest count falls between sizes?**
Rounding up is generally the safer choice, since running out of cake is a more noticeable issue than having extra left over.

**Does a 2kg cake cost exactly double a 1kg cake?**
Not always exactly double — pricing can vary slightly based on flavor and design, but weight remains the primary driver of cost difference between the two sizes.
