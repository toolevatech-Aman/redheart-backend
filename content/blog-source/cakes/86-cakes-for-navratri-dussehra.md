# Cakes for Navratri and Dussehra

Navratri and Dussehra mark a significant festive stretch in much of India, and while the occasion isn't traditionally cake-centric, modern gifting has extended into this period the same way it has into most major Indian festivals.

## Choosing a Flavor

Broadly celebratory flavors — chocolate, Black Forest — suit the occasion well, without needing a specific festival-coded flavor choice. Since Navratri involves fasting for many observers, checking dietary preferences before ordering is worth considering, similar to the eggless default that applies across much of Indian festival gifting.

## Pairing With Festival-Appropriate Flowers

Warm, celebratory tones in flowers pair naturally with a cake for this occasion, following the same combination logic used for other major Indian festivals.
