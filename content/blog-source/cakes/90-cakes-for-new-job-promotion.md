# Cakes for a New Job or Promotion

A cake works well as the celebratory element of a career milestone gift, particularly when paired with something more practically career-oriented like a desk plant.

## Why Cake Suits Career Celebrations

A cake turns a career milestone into a genuine celebration rather than just an acknowledgment, similar to how it functions for birthdays. This is particularly effective for a first day in a new role or a promotion announcement shared with a team.

## Pairing With a Practical Element

Following the same logic used for broader new job gifting, pairing a cake with a desk plant or a small practical item rounds out the gesture — the cake celebrates the moment, the plant furnishes the new role.

## Scaling to the Occasion

A modest cake suits a smaller, individual celebration; a larger cake works well for a team-wide celebration of someone's promotion or new role, similar to office birthday gifting conventions.

## Quick Answers

**Is cake an appropriate gift for a new job?**
Yes, particularly paired with a practical item like a desk plant — the cake celebrates the moment while the plant furnishes the new role.

**Should a promotion cake be shared with the team?**
For a team-wide celebration, a larger cake works well; for a more individual gesture, a smaller cake suits the occasion.
