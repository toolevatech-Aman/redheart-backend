# Cakes for Father's Day

Following the same bold-over-delicate logic that governs Father's Day flowers, cake choice for this occasion suits richer, more intense flavors better than a light, understated option.

## Choosing a Flavor

Chocolate truffle or a dense, rich chocolate option suits fathers well, mirroring the same bold aesthetic principle used in Father's Day flower and hamper gifting.

## Pairing With a Plant Instead of Flowers Alone

Since a bonsai or desk plant often suits fathers better than cut flowers, pairing a rich cake with a plant — rather than a floral bouquet — can create a more fitting combination for a father who might not naturally display flowers.
