# Anniversary Cakes: What to Order for Milestone Years

The same milestone-scaling logic that applies to anniversary flowers applies to cake choice too — early years call for classic romantic simplicity, while later milestones support something more elaborate.

## Early Anniversaries: Classic and Romantic

Red velvet is a strong choice for early anniversary years, echoing the same romantic register as red roses. A standard-sized cake (half to one kilogram) paired with a rose bouquet creates a complete, romantic gesture without needing to go elaborate this early.

## Mid-Range Anniversaries: A Step Up in Presentation

By the tenth year or so, a slightly larger cake or a more premium flavor — chocolate truffle rather than a simpler option — helps mark the occasion as more significant without requiring a dramatic change in approach.

## Milestone Anniversaries: Elaborate and Photo-Worthy

For 25th, 50th, and similar milestones, a larger, more elaborately designed cake — potentially multi-tiered, with custom decoration or a personalized message — suits the occasion's significance. This is a reasonable place to prioritize visual presentation alongside flavor, since milestone anniversary cakes are often photographed and remembered as part of the celebration itself.

## Pairing With Flowers for a Complete Gift

An anniversary cake paired with roses (red for a romantic register, or a broader mixed arrangement for a more milestone-scaled gift) creates the same complete celebratory tableau that works well for birthdays — the cake carries the celebration, the flowers carry the sentiment.

## Quick Answers

**What cake flavor suits an anniversary best?**
Red velvet is a common choice for its romantic association, particularly for earlier anniversary years, while richer options like chocolate truffle suit later milestones well.

**Should anniversary cakes get larger with each milestone?**
Generally, scaling size and elaborateness with milestone significance (25th, 50th) makes sense, similar to how flower arrangements are often scaled for anniversaries.

**Is it worth pairing an anniversary cake with flowers?**
Yes — the combination creates a more complete celebratory gift than either element alone, following the same logic that applies to birthday cake-and-flower pairings.
