# Tiered Cakes: When Do You Actually Need One?

Multi-tier cakes make a strong visual statement, but they're not automatically the right choice just because an occasion feels significant — practical serving needs should drive the decision as much as visual ambition.

## When a Tiered Cake Makes Sense

**Large guest counts.** A tiered cake naturally accommodates a higher total weight and serving capacity than a practical single-tier cake, making it a reasonable choice once guest counts move well into the dozens.

**Milestone occasions where visual impact matters.** Weddings, milestone anniversaries, and major celebrations benefit from a tiered cake's dramatic presentation, which a single-tier cake, however large, doesn't quite replicate.

## When a Single-Tier Cake Is the Better Choice

For most standard occasions — a routine birthday, a small family gathering — a single-tier cake, even at a larger size, is simpler to order, transport, and serve without sacrificing much in taste or presentation for a moderately sized event.

**Cost efficiency matters here too.** Tiered cakes generally cost more than a single-tier cake of equivalent total weight, given the added structural and decorative complexity — a consideration worth weighing against how much the visual drama actually matters for the specific occasion.

## Quick Answers

**When should I order a tiered cake instead of a single-tier one?**
For large guest counts or milestone occasions where visual impact matters significantly, such as weddings or major anniversaries.

**Are tiered cakes more expensive than single-tier cakes?**
Generally yes, given the added structural and decorative complexity involved in building multiple tiers.
