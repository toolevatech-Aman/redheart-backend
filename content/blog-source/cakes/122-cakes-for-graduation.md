# Cakes for Graduation

Following the same scale-to-significance logic used for graduation flowers, a graduation cake can reasonably go bigger and more elaborate than routine congratulations gifting, given the years of effort the occasion represents.

## Matching Cake to the Type of Graduation

Bright, festive cakes suit school and undergraduate graduations, where the mood tends to be exuberant, while a more composed, elegant cake suits postgraduate or professional program completions, mirroring the same distinction used in graduation flower gifting.

## Choosing a Design

A themed or personalized cake — referencing the school, degree, or field of study — adds a layer of specificity beyond a standard celebratory design, similar to how a specific message card adds warmth to graduation flowers.

## Pairing With Flowers for a Complete Gift

A cake paired with sunflowers or a bright mixed bouquet creates a complete graduation gift, echoing the achievement-signaling combination logic used across other congratulations-style occasions.

## Quick Answers

**Should a graduation cake be larger than a typical congratulations cake?**
Generally yes — the years of accumulated effort a graduation represents supports a larger, more substantial cake than a routine congratulations gesture.

**What design suits a graduation cake?**
A themed or personalized design referencing the school or field of study adds meaningful specificity beyond a standard celebratory cake.
