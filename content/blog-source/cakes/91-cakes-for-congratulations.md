# Cakes for Congratulations and Achievements

Scaling a congratulations cake to the size of the achievement follows the same logic that applies to congratulations flowers — a small win calls for restraint, a major milestone supports something more elaborate.

## Matching Cake to Achievement Size

A modest cake suits a smaller win — a good performance review, a minor personal milestone — without overstating the moment. A larger, more elaborately designed cake suits a genuinely significant achievement, similar to how graduation cakes can reasonably go bigger than routine congratulations gifting.

## Flavor Considerations

Broadly appealing flavors work well here, since the achievement itself carries the occasion's significance rather than needing a specific flavor association. Personalizing with a message referencing the specific achievement adds more warmth than a generic celebratory design.

## Pairing With Flowers

A cake paired with sunflowers or bright mixed flowers — following the same achievement-signaling logic used in congratulations flower gifting — creates a complete celebratory gift.
