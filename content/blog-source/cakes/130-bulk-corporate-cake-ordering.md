# Bulk and Corporate Cake Ordering for Events

Ordering cake for a larger event — a company party, a conference, a large family function — involves different planning considerations than a standard individual gift order.

## Sizing for Larger Groups

For events with dozens or more attendees, multiple mid-sized cakes often work better than a single very large cake, since they're easier to serve simultaneously across a larger space and offer flavor variety for a bigger, more varied group.

## Planning Lead Time for Bulk Orders

Larger orders generally benefit from more advance notice than a standard individual order, since bulk preparation requires more planning than a single standard-sized cake — worth ordering well ahead of the event date rather than assuming bulk orders can be arranged with the same short notice as an individual cake.

## Flavor Selection for Mixed Groups

Broadly appealing, neutral flavors work best for large, mixed groups, following the same safe-default logic used for unfamiliar or professional gifting — this isn't the context for a single novel or polarizing flavor choice, given the wider range of preferences across a large group.

## Dietary Considerations at Scale

For a large event, offering both egg and eggless options — rather than a single choice for the entire order — accommodates a wider range of dietary practices across a mixed group, similar to the eggless-default logic that applies to smaller group gifting.

## Quick Answers

**Should I order one large cake or multiple smaller ones for an event?**
Multiple mid-sized cakes often work better for larger events, offering easier serving logistics and flavor variety for a bigger, more varied group.

**How far in advance should I place a bulk cake order?**
Generally further ahead than an individual order, since bulk preparation requires more planning time than a standard-sized cake.
