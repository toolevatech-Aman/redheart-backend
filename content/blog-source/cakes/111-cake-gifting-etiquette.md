# Cake Gifting Etiquette: Do's and Don'ts

A few small missteps come up repeatedly in cake gifting, most of them easily avoided with a bit of forethought about the recipient's actual situation rather than assumptions about what should work.

## Do: Check Dietary Requirements Before Ordering

Given how common eggless requirements are across Indian households, confirming this detail before finalizing an order avoids sending a cake the recipient can't eat — one of the more common and entirely avoidable cake-gifting mistakes.

## Don't: Assume Everyone Wants an Elaborate, Photo-Worthy Cake

Not every occasion calls for maximalist presentation — matching the cake's scale and elaborateness to the actual significance of the occasion, rather than defaulting to the most elaborate option available, tends to land better and avoid feeling disproportionate.

## Do: Time the Delivery to the Actual Moment

Whether it's a same-day delivery for a spontaneous gesture or a planned midnight delivery for a birthday surprise, timing matters — a cake arriving well before or after the actual celebratory moment loses some of its impact compared to one that arrives right when it's meant to.

## Don't: Default to Red Velvet Outside Romantic Contexts

Red velvet's strong romantic association can feel mismatched in non-romantic or professional gifting contexts, similar to how red roses can feel out of place outside a romantic relationship — a neutral flavor generally suits these situations better.

## Do: Consider Pairing Cake With Something Else for Significant Occasions

For milestone birthdays, anniversaries, or major celebrations, a cake paired with flowers or a plant tends to create a more complete, memorable gift than cake alone, which functions well as a standalone gesture mainly for more routine occasions.

## Quick Answers

**What's the most common cake gifting mistake?**
Forgetting to confirm dietary requirements, particularly eggless preferences, which are common enough in Indian households to be worth checking before every order.

**Is red velvet appropriate for non-romantic gifting?**
Generally not the best choice, given its strong romantic association — a neutral flavor like chocolate or butterscotch tends to suit non-romantic contexts better.
