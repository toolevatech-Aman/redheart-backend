# Cake Sizes Explained: How Many People Does Each Size Serve?

Ordering a cake that's too small for the gathering, or far larger than needed, is one of the more common and avoidable mistakes in online cake ordering. Weight-to-serving ratios are fairly consistent across most bakeries, which makes this easier to plan around than it might seem.

## Half Kilogram: Small, Personal Celebrations

A half-kilogram cake typically serves around 4 to 6 people, depending on slice size. This is the right choice for a small family celebration, a quiet birthday, or any occasion where the cake is a personal gesture rather than something meant to serve a larger group.

## One Kilogram: The Standard Choice

A one-kilogram cake generally serves 8 to 10 people, making it the most common size for a standard birthday gathering, a small office celebration, or a modest family party. This is the default size for most routine cake orders, balancing enough servings for a moderate group without excess.

## Two Kilograms: Larger Gatherings

A two-kilogram cake typically serves around 15 to 20 people, suited to a larger birthday party, a bigger family gathering, or an office celebration involving a full team rather than a small group. This size also tends to make more visual impact as a centerpiece, given its larger footprint.

## Three Kilograms and Above: Major Events

Cakes at three kilograms and beyond generally serve 25 or more people, and are typically reserved for weddings, large milestone celebrations, or major events where the cake is expected to be both a significant visual centerpiece and a genuine dessert course for a large guest list.

## Factors That Affect These Estimates

**Slice size assumptions vary.** The serving estimates above assume standard-sized slices; if the cake is one part of a larger dessert spread (alongside other sweets or a buffet), actual servings can stretch further, since guests often take smaller portions when more options are available.

**Multi-tier cakes serve differently than single-tier cakes of the same total weight**, since tiered cakes often have a higher ratio of decorative fondant or icing to actual cake, which can reduce effective servings slightly compared to a single dense tier of the same weight.

**Rich, dense cakes (like a full chocolate truffle) are sometimes served in smaller slices than lighter cakes**, since richness can mean guests naturally take less per slice, effectively stretching the serving count slightly beyond the standard estimate.

## A Simple Way to Choose

- **Small family gathering (4–6 people)** → half kilogram
- **Standard birthday party (8–10 people)** → one kilogram
- **Larger celebration or office party (15–20 people)** → two kilograms
- **Wedding or major event (25+ people)** → three kilograms or above

If the guest count falls between two size tiers, it's generally safer to size up slightly rather than down — running short on cake at a celebration is a more noticeable shortfall than having a modest amount left over.

## Should You Order Multiple Smaller Cakes Instead of One Large One?

For some occasions, ordering two or three smaller cakes in different flavors can work better than one large cake of a single flavor — this gives guests a choice and can feel more generous for a similar total cost, though it sacrifices the single-centerpiece visual impact that one larger cake provides. This is worth considering for gatherings where guests are likely to have different flavor preferences.

## Quick Answers

**How many people does a 1kg cake serve?**
Generally around 8 to 10 people, assuming standard slice sizes — this is the most common size for a typical birthday gathering.

**Is it better to order a bigger cake than I think I need?**
Slightly oversizing is usually the safer choice, since running out of cake at a celebration is a more noticeable issue than having some left over.

**Do multi-tier cakes serve the same number of people as a single-tier cake of equal weight?**
Not always — multi-tier cakes often have a higher ratio of decorative icing to cake, which can slightly reduce effective servings compared to a single dense tier of the same weight.

**Should I order one large cake or multiple smaller ones for a big party?**
It depends on your priorities — multiple smaller cakes offer flavor variety and can feel generous, while one larger cake provides a stronger single visual centerpiece.
