# Cake for Two: Small-Batch Cakes for Intimate Celebrations

Not every celebration involves a group — for a quiet anniversary dinner or a small, private birthday, a smaller cake suited to just one or two people avoids the waste and excess of a standard-sized order.

## Why Standard Sizing Doesn't Always Fit

A half-kilogram cake, the smallest standard size at most bakeries, still generally serves 4 to 6 people — more than enough for an intimate celebration, and often resulting in more leftover cake than a couple or individual actually wants to manage afterward.

## When a Smaller, Intimate Cake Makes Sense

**Quiet anniversary dinners**, where the celebration is intentionally private rather than a larger gathering.

**Solo birthday celebrations**, where a small, personal cake still marks the occasion without requiring a size meant for sharing with a larger group.

**Any occasion where scale is intentionally kept modest**, prioritizing quality and presentation over serving capacity.

## Choosing Flavor and Presentation for a Smaller Occasion

Since a smaller cake serves fewer purposes beyond direct enjoyment (no need to stretch servings across many guests), it's a reasonable occasion to prioritize a richer, more premium flavor — chocolate truffle, for instance — over a broadly crowd-pleasing option chosen for wide appeal.

## Quick Answers

**Is it worth ordering a full-sized cake for just two people?**
Not necessarily — a half-kilogram cake, while still relatively small by standard sizing, may still be more than needed; checking for smaller options suited to an intimate celebration is worth considering.

**Should an intimate cake be a special or standard flavor?**
Since serving capacity matters less for a small, private occasion, it's a good opportunity to prioritize a richer, more premium flavor choice.
