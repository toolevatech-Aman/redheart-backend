# Cakes for Welcoming a New Colleague

While a desk plant remains the more practical, lasting choice for welcoming a new team member, a small cake can add a warmer, more immediately celebratory element to a first-day welcome.

## When a Cake Fits Better Than a Plant

For a team-wide welcome gathering, a small shared cake creates a natural moment for the team to meet the new colleague together, something a desk plant alone doesn't facilitate in the same way.

## Choosing a Flavor for Office Settings

Broadly appealing, neutral flavors — chocolate, Black Forest, or butterscotch — suit a mixed office group well, avoiding anything overly rich or polarizing for a first-impression occasion.

## Pairing With a Plant for a Complete Welcome

Combining a small shared cake for the team moment with a desk plant for the new colleague's ongoing workspace covers both the immediate welcome and the lasting gesture, mirroring the combination logic used across other office gifting occasions.

## Quick Answers

**Is cake an appropriate way to welcome a new colleague?**
Yes, particularly for a team-wide gathering — it creates a shared moment that a desk plant alone doesn't provide.

**Should I pair a welcome cake with a plant?**
Often yes — the cake covers the immediate team welcome, while a desk plant provides a lasting gesture for the new colleague's workspace.
