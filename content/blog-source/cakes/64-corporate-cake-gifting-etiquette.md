# Corporate Cake Gifting: Choosing Flavors and Presentation for Colleagues and Clients

Office cake gifting benefits from the same restraint that applies to corporate flowers — neutral, well-liked flavors over anything personal or romantically coded.

## Flavor Choices That Work Professionally

Butterscotch and plain chocolate are the safer defaults for colleagues and clients, avoiding red velvet's romantic association. Black Forest also works well, offering broad appeal without any specific relationship-coded meaning attached.

## Sizing for Office Celebrations

A one- to two-kilogram cake generally suits a team-sized office celebration, while a smaller half-kilogram cake works for a more individual, low-key colleague gesture — matching size to the scale of the celebration avoids either an underwhelming or excessive presentation.

## Pairing With Other Professional Gifts

For client-facing or executive gifting, a neutral-flavored cake paired with premium chocolates and a tasteful flower arrangement creates a well-rounded professional hamper, following the same combination logic used for corporate flower gifting generally.

## What to Avoid

Red velvet, given its romantic association, is generally worth avoiding for professional gifting contexts, along with overly elaborate or novelty designs that can feel mismatched to a workplace setting.

## Quick Answers

**What cake flavor is best for a colleague's birthday?**
Butterscotch, plain chocolate, or Black Forest are generally the safest, most broadly appropriate choices for professional gifting.

**Should I avoid red velvet cake for office gifting?**
Generally yes, given its strong romantic association, which can feel mismatched in most professional contexts.

**What size cake suits an office celebration?**
A one- to two-kilogram cake generally suits a team celebration, while a smaller size works for an individual colleague gesture.
