# Cakes for a Friend

Cake gifting for friends has the same color-and-flavor freedom that governs friend-directed flowers — with no romantic ambiguity to navigate, the choice can follow personality rather than convention.

## Avoiding Romantically-Coded Flavors

Red velvet, given its strong romantic association, is worth avoiding for a platonic friend gift, mirroring the same logic that applies to red roses in friendship gifting — a broadly appealing option like chocolate or Black Forest is generally the safer, clearer choice.

## Matching Flavor to Occasion

Birthdays, congratulations, or an unprompted "just because" cake all work well for a friend, following the same range of occasions appropriate for friend-directed flower gifting.

## Pairing With Bright, Cheerful Flowers

A cake paired with gerberas or a bright mixed bouquet creates a warm, clearly platonic celebratory gift, mirroring the combination logic used throughout friend gifting.

## Quick Answers

**Is red velvet appropriate for a friend's cake?**
Generally not the best choice, given its romantic association — a neutral flavor like chocolate is a clearer, safer choice for platonic gifting.

**What occasions suit a friend's cake?**
Birthdays, congratulations, or an unprompted delivery all work well, since friend gifting isn't limited to one specific occasion.
