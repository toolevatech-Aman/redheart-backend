# Surat's Trade-Success Cake Gifting

Surat's prominence in diamond and textile trade has built a cake culture where marking business success is genuinely woven into local celebration habits.

## Cake as a Marker of Commercial Achievement
Given how central trade success is to Surat's civic identity, cake gifting for business milestones carries real cultural weight, distinct from purely personal occasion-based gifting.

## Extended Gujarati Festival Season
Consistent with Ahmedabad, Surat's cake demand stays elevated across the Navratri-to-Bestu Varas window, reflecting the shared regional festival calendar.
