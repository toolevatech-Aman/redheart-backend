Coimbatore's identity as part of the historic Kongu Nadu region gives the city its own cultural character within Tamil Nadu, and cake here has found a genuine place alongside the region's traditional sweets.

Traditional Tamil sweets remain central to Coimbatore's celebratory food culture, with cake typically taking the specific role of the birthday candle-blowing centerpiece rather than displacing traditional mithai from broader celebrations.

## Navratri Golu's Local Practice
