# Jaipur's Wedding-Scale Cake Industry

Jaipur's status as a premier destination wedding location has built a cake industry capable of handling elaborate, large-scale orders well beyond routine city demand.

## Cakes Built for Palace Wedding Venues
Given how many weddings Jaipur hosts against its palace and fort backdrop, local cake suppliers routinely produce large, multi-tier designs matching the grandeur of these settings.

## Everyday Demand Beyond Weddings
Outside wedding season, Jaipur's cake culture follows more standard personal and festival gifting conventions, with chocolate and red velvet remaining reliably popular.
