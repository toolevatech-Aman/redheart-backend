# Mumbai's Bakery Heritage and What It Means for Cake Choice

Mumbai's cake culture didn't start with online delivery — the city has a genuine bakery lineage stretching back generations, shaped in particular by its historic Irani cafés.

## The Irani Café Legacy

Mumbai's Irani cafés, established by Zoroastrian immigrants from Iran starting in the early 20th century, brought a distinct baking tradition into the city — buns, biscuits, and baked goods that became woven into Mumbai's everyday food culture long before modern cake delivery existed. This legacy means the city has an unusually long, layered relationship with baked goods compared to many other Indian cities.

## A City Comfortable With Both Old and New Baking Traditions

Mumbai residents move easily between the old-world charm of a legacy Irani café and a slickly designed, app-ordered photo cake — the city doesn't treat these as competing traditions so much as different tools for different moments, reflecting Mumbai's broader comfort holding multiple identities at once.

## What Moves in the Highest Volume

Chocolate truffle and Black Forest remain the most consistently popular cake choices in Mumbai, with red velvet finding strong demand given the city's substantial romantic and anniversary gifting culture, echoed by its Bollywood-influenced flower preferences.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Given Mumbai's genuine bakery heritage, quality and proper texture matter here — a city with this much baking history has residents who can tell the difference between a well-made cake and a purely photogenic one, even amid the fast-paced, delivery-driven culture that dominates day-to-day ordering.
