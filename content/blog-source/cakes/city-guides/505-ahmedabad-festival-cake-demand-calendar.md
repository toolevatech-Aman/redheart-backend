# Ahmedabad's Festival-Driven Cake Demand Calendar

Given how dense Gujarat's festival calendar is, cake demand in Ahmedabad follows a genuinely different rhythm than cities with a single major festival peak.

## Extended Demand Across Navratri Through Diwali
Rather than concentrating around one occasion, Ahmedabad's cake demand stays elevated across the extended Navratri-to-Bestu Varas window, reflecting the region's dense, sequential festival calendar.

## Business Milestone Cake Gifting
Given the city's strong trade culture, cake has also become a natural way to mark business achievements, sitting alongside its extensive festival-driven demand.

## Quick Answers

**Why does Ahmedabad's cake demand span an extended period?**
Gujarat's dense, sequential festival calendar — Navratri through Diwali and Bestu Varas — keeps demand elevated across weeks rather than a single peak day.

**Is business-related cake gifting significant in Ahmedabad?**
Yes — given the city's strong trade culture, cake has become a natural way to mark business achievements.
