# Kolkata's Craft-Conscious Cake Buyers

Kolkata's exceptionally refined sweets tradition has cultivated a population that genuinely notices the difference between a well-crafted cake and an ordinary one.

## A City That Expects Genuine Craft
Given how sophisticated Bengali sweets like rosogolla and sandesh are, Kolkata buyers tend to hold cake to a similarly high standard — flavor and texture matter more here than presentation alone.

## Durga Puja Season Demand
Cake demand rises notably during the extended Durga Puja season, alongside the city's already substantial traditional sweets consumption during this period.
