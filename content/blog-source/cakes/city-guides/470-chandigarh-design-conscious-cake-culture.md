# Chandigarh's Design-Conscious Cake Culture

Chandigarh's identity as India's first planned city, designed by Le Corbusier, extends into a genuine appreciation for well-composed, thoughtfully presented cakes.

## A City That Values Deliberate Design
Given Chandigarh's founding design philosophy of order and intentional planning, a well-composed cake tends to suit local sensibility better than a purely functional option.

## Punjab-Haryana's Generous Celebration Culture
As the shared capital of Punjab and Haryana, cake gifting here favors substantial, generously presented choices, consistent with the region's broader hospitality traditions.
