# Fixed Time Delivery Explained: When Same-Day Isn't Precise Enough

Same-day delivery answers "will it arrive today," but not "will it arrive at 3 PM specifically." For most gifting occasions that gap doesn't matter. For some, it's the entire point of the gesture — and that's the specific gap Fixed Time Delivery is built to close.

## What Fixed Time Delivery Actually Solves

Same-day delivery gives a broad window — the flowers will arrive sometime during the day, but not at a guaranteed hour. Midnight delivery solves timing precision for exactly one moment: the stroke of 12. Neither option helps if what you actually need is a delivery at, say, 2 PM sharp for a surprise office celebration, or 7 PM to coincide with someone getting home from work. Fixed Time Delivery (₹199) locks in a specific delivery hour, closing that gap for a modest additional charge.

## When the Extra Cost Is Worth It

**Surprise moments tied to a specific event.** If flowers need to arrive exactly when a birthday lunch starts, or precisely as a video call is scheduled to begin, a broad same-day window creates real risk of the surprise landing too early or too late. Fixed Time Delivery removes that uncertainty for a fixed, modest fee.

**Coordinating with someone else's schedule.** If you know the recipient is only reliably home or at their desk during a specific hour, timing the delivery to that window avoids the flowers arriving when no one's there to receive them, which matters more for time-sensitive occasions than for a routine same-day gift.

**Public or workplace deliveries meant to be witnessed.** A bouquet arriving during a specific meeting, or right as colleagues gather for a birthday toast, has more impact if the timing is exact rather than "sometime this afternoon."

## When Standard Same-Day Delivery Is Enough

For most occasions, the extra precision isn't necessary. A same-day delivery for a routine birthday, a get well soon gesture, or an unprompted "just because" bouquet doesn't lose much by arriving within a broader window rather than a specific hour — the sentiment lands the same way regardless of the exact minute.

Fixed Time Delivery is worth reserving for occasions where the timing itself carries meaning, rather than defaulting to it for every order — the additional ₹199 is well spent when precision matters and unnecessary when it doesn't.

## How This Compares to Midnight Delivery

Midnight delivery is, in a sense, a specific case of fixed-time delivery — it locks in one particular window (11 PM–12 AM) built around the specific emotional logic of a day beginning. Fixed Time Delivery generalizes that same idea to any hour of the day, for occasions where a different specific moment matters more than midnight does.

## Ordering Considerations

As with midnight delivery, Fixed Time Delivery benefits from being planned rather than decided at the last minute — locking in a specific hour is easier to guarantee with some lead time than with a same-day, last-minute request. It's worth selecting this option deliberately at checkout, rather than assuming standard same-day delivery will happen to land at the right moment.
