# Surat's Flower Sourcing and What It Tells You About Local Taste

Unlike some cities with one dominant central flower market, Surat's flower supply draws more directly from nearby growing regions, giving the city's flower trade a slightly different character worth understanding.

## The Anand-Kheda Belt Connection

Surat's flower supply draws significantly from the Anand-Kheda growing belt elsewhere in Gujarat, rather than centering entirely on a single iconic city market the way some other major cities do. This regional sourcing pattern reflects Gujarat's broader agricultural strength in flower cultivation, feeding multiple cities across the state rather than concentrating around one urban hub.

## What Moves Through in the Highest Volume

Marigolds, tuberose, roses, chrysanthemums, and seasonal mixed flowers dominate Surat's flower trade — a mix closely aligned with Ahmedabad's and broader Gujarati flower culture, given the shared growing regions supplying both cities.

## Why Regional Sourcing Matters for Freshness

Sourcing from a nearby growing belt rather than a distant supply chain means flowers reaching Surat retailers and delivery services travel a relatively short distance from cultivation to sale — a genuine freshness advantage for same-day delivery, since the flowers haven't spent extended time in transit before even reaching the local market.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Marigold and tuberose, given their prominence in Gujarat's growing regions and the state's festival calendar, tend to be the most reliably fresh and available choices for delivery in Surat.
