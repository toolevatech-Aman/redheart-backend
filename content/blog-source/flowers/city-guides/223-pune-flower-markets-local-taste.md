# Pune's Flower Markets and What They Tell You About Local Taste

Pune's flower trade centers on a market that supplies both the city's traditional festival demand and its growing modern gifting culture, reflecting Pune's broader identity as a city balancing heritage with rapid growth.

## Gultekdi Market: Pune's Central Flower Hub

Gultekdi Market functions as Pune's primary wholesale flower hub, supplying retail florists and delivery services across the city's expanding footprint, from the older central neighborhoods to newer IT-corridor areas like Hinjewadi and Kharadi.

## What Moves Through in the Highest Volume

Roses, marigolds, gerberas, sunflowers, and mixed bouquets dominate Pune's flower trade — a mix that leans toward contemporary, romantic, and casual celebratory gifting more than heavily traditional temple-oriented flower use, reflecting Pune's younger, more cosmopolitan gifting base.

## Supplying a City That's Grown Rapidly

Pune's expansion into new residential and commercial areas over recent decades — Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Wakad, Baner — has extended the reach flower delivery needs to cover, and markets like Gultekdi have adapted to supply this broader geographic footprint alongside the city's older core.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Given how much of Pune's flower volume centers on roses, gerberas, and sunflowers rather than more traditional varieties, these tend to be the most reliably fresh and available choices for delivery across the city, including in the newer IT-corridor neighborhoods that have grown substantially in recent years.
