# Why Flower Gifting Means So Much in Jaipur

Jaipur's occasion calendar carries the distinct imprint of Rajasthani culture, with festivals unique to the region shaping a flower-gifting tradition unlike almost anywhere else in North India.

## Teej: A Festival Built on Celebration and Adornment

Teej stands as one of Rajasthan's most significant festivals, celebrated with particular intensity in Jaipur and centered on themes of marital happiness, adornment, and monsoon celebration — a festival with a flower-gifting character distinct from more commonly known North Indian occasions.

## Gangaur's Regional Significance

Gangaur, another festival closely associated with Rajasthan specifically, adds further regional depth to Jaipur's occasion calendar, reflecting the state's strong tradition of festivals centered on marital devotion and community celebration.

## A City Where Marigold Carries Extra Weight

Given how many of Jaipur's major festivals — Teej, Gangaur, Diwali — lean on traditional devotional and celebratory flower use, marigold's prominence in the city's flower culture (referenced locally as genda, including for garlands) reflects genuinely sustained demand tied to this dense regional festival calendar.

## Weddings in the City Known for Grand Celebration

Jaipur's reputation as a premier wedding destination in India adds a further layer to its flower-gifting culture — the city's flower trade regularly supports both resident and destination weddings, a scale of floral demand that shapes the local market beyond what its population alone might suggest.

## What This Means for Choosing a Gift

Jaipur's occasion calendar leans more heavily on distinctly Rajasthani festivals than many other major Indian cities, making it worth choosing flowers and timing with these specific regional occasions — Teej and Gangaur especially — rather than relying solely on a national festival calendar.
