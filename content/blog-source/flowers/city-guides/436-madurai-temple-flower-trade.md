# Madurai's Temple Flower Trade and What It Reveals About the City

Given how central the Meenakshi Amman Temple is to Madurai's daily life, the city's flower trade runs on a rhythm shaped by temple demand as much as by gifting occasions.

## A Trade Built Around Continuous Temple Offerings

Unlike cities where flower demand spikes mainly around specific festivals, Madurai's temple-centered daily life sustains a steady, year-round baseline of flower demand tied to offerings and decoration — jasmine and lotus moving through local trade in volumes that reflect this continuous devotional use rather than isolated seasonal peaks.

## Madurai Malli as a Local Identity Marker

The city's specific jasmine variety, Madurai Malli, has developed an identity distinct enough that it functions almost as a regional product in its own right — grown and traded with a reputation that extends beyond the city itself, similar to how certain agricultural products become associated with a specific place of origin.
