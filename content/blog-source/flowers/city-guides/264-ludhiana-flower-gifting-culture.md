Ludhiana's identity as the hosiery and textile capital of Asia, and its role as the origin city of Hero Cycles, gives the city a strong industrial and entrepreneurial character that runs alongside Punjab's rich festival traditions.

## Lohri's Special Place in Punjab's Biggest Industrial City

Lohri carries particular cultural weight in Ludhiana, consistent with the festival's deep roots in Punjab more broadly, marking the winter harvest with the warmth and community gathering the occasion is known for across the state.

## Baisakhi and the Punjabi Harvest Calendar

Baisakhi holds major significance in Ludhiana's festival calendar, tied to Punjab's agricultural traditions and the Sikh religious calendar, reflecting the city's strong connection to the state's broader cultural and religious identity.

## Gurpurabs Reflecting the City's Sikh Heritage

Gurpurabs, commemorating significant events in Sikh history, hold genuine significance in Ludhiana's gifting calendar, consistent with Punjab's strong Sikh cultural and religious presence.
