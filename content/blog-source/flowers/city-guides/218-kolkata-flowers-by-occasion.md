# Best Flowers for Every Occasion in Kolkata

Kolkata's occasion calendar is shaped heavily by an extended festival season, and choosing the right flower for each occasion matters given how much cultural weight the city's biggest celebrations carry.

## Durga Puja

Marigold and other festival-appropriate flowers suit the occasion's elaborate decorative traditions well, particularly for gifting tied to pandal-visiting season or family gatherings during the festival. Shiuli's seasonal appearance around this time makes it a particularly fitting, culturally resonant choice if available.

## Kali Puja

Following closely after Durga Puja, similar traditional flower choices suit Kali Puja's continuation of the festival season, with red flowers carrying particular significance given the occasion's association with Goddess Kali.

## Saraswati Puja

Yellow flowers hold specific significance for Saraswati Puja, tied to the goddess's traditional association with the color and the arrival of spring the festival marks.

## Rabindra Jayanti

A more restrained, culturally thoughtful arrangement suits this occasion's literary and artistic character better than an overtly festive or romantic bouquet — this is a day about cultural reflection rather than celebration in the more conventional sense.

## Birthdays and Anniversaries

Roses and mixed bouquets suit Kolkata's personal celebratory gifting, following similar logic to birthday and anniversary gifting nationally, sitting comfortably alongside the city's festival-heavy calendar rather than competing with it.

## Valentine's Day

Red roses remain the standard choice, with Kolkata's strong appreciation for artistic presentation meaning a well-composed, thoughtfully arranged bouquet tends to be particularly well received here.
