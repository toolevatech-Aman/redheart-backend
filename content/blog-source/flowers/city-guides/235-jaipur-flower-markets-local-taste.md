# Jaipur's Flower Markets and What They Tell You About Local Taste

Jaipur's flower trade centers on a historic market located in the heart of the old Pink City, reflecting how deeply flower trading is woven into the city's traditional commercial fabric.

## Chandpole Bazaar: Flowers in the Old City

Chandpole Bazaar functions as Jaipur's primary flower market, situated within the historic walled city — a location that places the flower trade directly alongside Jaipur's other traditional bazaars and commercial districts, rather than in a separate modern commercial zone.

## What Moves Through in the Highest Volume

Roses, marigolds, jasmine, tuberose, and marigold garlands (genda) dominate Jaipur's flower trade, alongside mixed bouquets for more contemporary gifting. This mix reflects the city's dual demand — traditional devotional and festival use alongside modern romantic and celebratory gifting.

## Supplying a City Built for Grand Celebration

Given Jaipur's status as a major wedding destination, Chandpole Bazaar and the broader local flower trade regularly handle demand well beyond what the resident population alone would generate — a genuine scale advantage that supports strong availability and freshness even during the city's busiest wedding and festival periods.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Marigold, roses, and jasmine tend to be the most reliably fresh and available choices for delivery in Jaipur, given how central these varieties are to both the city's everyday devotional use and its substantial wedding and festival trade.
