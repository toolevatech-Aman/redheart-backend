Pune's occasion calendar blends Maharashtrian festival traditions with a strong contemporary romantic and college-town gifting culture — matching flower choice to occasion helps navigate this genuine variety.

Marigold and warm-toned flowers suit the festival's decorative and devotional use, following similar logic to Ganesh Chaturthi gifting in Mumbai, with demand spiking significantly during the celebration period across the city.

## Navratri

Traditional flowers appropriate for Maharashtrian Navratri observance suit the occasion's regional cultural significance, particularly for gifting to family and friends actively participating in the festival's traditions.

## College Fests

Bright, playful arrangements — gerberas or colorful mixed bouquets — suit the more casual, celebratory tone of Pune's significant college-fest gifting category, distinct from the more restrained choices appropriate for other occasions.

## Birthdays

Roses, sunflowers, and mixed bouquets suit Pune's younger, personality-driven birthday gifting culture, following similar logic to birthday gifting nationally but with a demographic skew toward a younger population.

## Anniversaries

Red roses remain the standard for romantic milestones, with Pune's cosmopolitan, Mumbai-adjacent character supporting both classic and more contemporary arrangement styles.

## Valentine's Day

Given Pune's large student and young-professional population, Valentine's Day generates strong demand across a range of price points and styles, from budget-friendly options to more premium romantic arrangements.
