# Kolkata's Flower Markets and What They Tell You About Local Taste

Kolkata's flower trade centers on one of the most historically significant markets in the country, situated in a location that reflects the city's own layered history.

## Mullick Ghat Flower Market: A Market Rooted in the River

Located along the Hooghly River near Howrah Bridge, Mullick Ghat Flower Market is one of the oldest and largest flower markets in India, operating in a setting that itself has become a recognizable symbol of the city. The market's riverside location historically supported both religious offerings and the trade routes that brought flowers into Kolkata from surrounding growing regions.

## What Moves Through in the Highest Volume

Tuberose (rajnigandha), roses, marigolds, lotus, shiuli (night jasmine), and gladioli dominate Kolkata's flower trade — a distinctive mix that includes shiuli, a flower strongly associated with the arrival of autumn and Durga Puja specifically, giving Kolkata's flower culture a seasonal character less pronounced in cities with a more consistent year-round variety.

## Shiuli as a Cultural Marker

Shiuli's association with the transition into autumn and the approach of Durga Puja makes it more than just a seasonal flower in Kolkata — its appearance functions almost as a cultural signal that the city's biggest festival season is beginning, giving it a symbolic weight that goes beyond its use in bouquets or offerings.

## Why This Matters for Sourcing During Festival Season

Given how central Durga Puja is to Kolkata's flower-gifting calendar, demand at Mullick Ghat spikes dramatically in the weeks surrounding the festival — worth factoring in if ordering flowers during this period, since availability and pricing can shift with the surge in citywide demand.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Tuberose, roses, and marigolds tend to be the most consistently available varieties for delivery in Kolkata outside the main festival season, while shiuli and certain premium varieties may see more seasonal fluctuation tied to Durga Puja timing specifically.
