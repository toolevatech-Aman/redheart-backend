## What Delhi Tends to Order

Red roses, tuberose (commonly known locally as rajnigandha), marigold, orchids, and lilies are the most consistently ordered flowers in Delhi. This mix reflects the city's dual gifting culture — tuberose and marigold lean into traditional festival and wedding use, while roses and orchids cover the more contemporary romantic and corporate gifting occasions.

Delhi's flower-buying calendar is unusually broad: Karva Chauth, Diwali, weddings, workplace promotions, Raksha Bandhan, birthdays, and Valentine's Day all show up as major occasions here, more evenly spread across festival and Western-style gifting than in some other cities.

## Where the Flowers Actually Come From

Fresh flower supply in Delhi runs through wholesale markets like Ghazipur Phool Mandi and INA Market, both supplied nightly by farms from as far as Pune and the Yamuna belt. Sourcing locally through these markets — rather than relying on flowers shipped in from elsewhere — is part of what keeps same-day delivery genuinely fresh rather than pre-arranged days in advance.
