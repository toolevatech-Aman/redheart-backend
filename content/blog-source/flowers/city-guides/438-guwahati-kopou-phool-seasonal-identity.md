# Guwahati's Kopou Phool and Its Seasonal Flower Identity

Few Indian cities have a flower as tightly bound to a single festival as Guwahati's relationship with Kopou Phool and Bihu.

## A Flower That Signals the Season Itself

Kopou Phool, the foxtail orchid, blooms in close alignment with Bihu's timing, making its appearance in local markets almost a seasonal marker in its own right — its presence signals the festival's approach the way certain flowers elsewhere signal spring or harvest season.

## Beyond Kopou Phool: A City Connected to the Northeast's Broader Flora

As the gateway to Northeast India, Guwahati's flower trade draws on the region's distinct biodiversity, giving the city access to varieties less commonly available in flower markets further west or south in India.
