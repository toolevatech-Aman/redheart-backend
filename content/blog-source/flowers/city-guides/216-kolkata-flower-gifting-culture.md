# Why Flower Gifting Means So Much in Kolkata

Kolkata's relationship with flowers is inseparable from its identity as a cultural and artistic capital — the city's biggest festivals are built around elaborate floral displays, and that scale shapes how the whole city thinks about flowers even outside festival season.

## Durga Puja: A Festival Built on Flowers

Durga Puja stands as the defining occasion in Kolkata's flower-gifting calendar, driving demand at a scale that shapes the city's flower markets and supply chains for weeks around the festival. The elaborate floral decoration of pandals across the city sets a visual standard that carries over into how Kolkata approaches flower gifting more broadly — generous, artistic, and detail-conscious.

## Kali Puja and Saraswati Puja Extending the Season

Following closely behind Durga Puja, Kali Puja and Saraswati Puja keep flower demand elevated across an extended festival season rather than concentrated into a single peak — a pattern distinct from cities where one major festival dominates and the rest of the year is comparatively quiet.

## Rabindra Jayanti and Kolkata's Literary-Cultural Identity

Rabindra Jayanti, marking the birth anniversary of Rabindranath Tagore, functions as a meaningful cultural occasion in Kolkata specifically, reflecting the city's strong identification with Bengali literary and artistic heritage — a occasion category less prominent in cities without Kolkata's particular cultural history.

## What This Means for Choosing a Gift

Kolkata's festival-heavy calendar means flower gifting here often carries more cultural and artistic weight than in cities where gifting is driven primarily by personal romantic or celebratory occasions — worth keeping in mind when choosing both the flower and the presentation.

**Why is Durga Puja so significant for Kolkata's flower culture?**
The festival's elaborate floral pandal decorations set a citywide standard for generous, artistic flower use that shapes gifting sensibilities well beyond the festival itself.

**Does Kolkata have an extended festival season for flowers?**
Yes — Durga Puja, Kali Puja, and Saraswati Puja follow in relatively close succession, keeping flower demand elevated across an extended period rather than one isolated peak.

**What makes Rabindra Jayanti significant in Kolkata specifically?**
It marks Rabindranath Tagore's birth anniversary and reflects the city's strong identification with Bengali literary and artistic heritage, a cultural occasion less prominent elsewhere.
