# Best Flowers for Every Occasion in Kochi

Kochi's occasion calendar spans genuinely distinct Hindu and Christian traditions, and choosing flowers appropriate to each occasion's specific cultural context matters more here than in cities with a single dominant festival calendar.

## Onam

Marigold, chrysanthemums, and a wide range of colorful flowers suit Onam gifting, particularly given the festival's association with the elaborate pookalam floral designs central to its celebration.

## Vishu

Traditional flowers suited to Kerala's New Year celebrations, including marigold and lotus, suit Vishu gifting, reflecting the occasion's devotional and celebratory character.

## Christmas

Following broader Christmas gifting conventions — red, white, and green tones, with roses and carnations — suits Kochi's substantial Christian population well, consistent with how the occasion is marked elsewhere.

## Easter

Lighter, softer flower choices — white lilies, pastel roses — suit Easter's themes of renewal and celebration, reflecting the occasion's genuine significance within Kochi's Christian community.

## Weddings

Given Kochi's mix of traditions, wedding flower choices vary meaningfully by the couple's specific background — jasmine and traditional South Indian wedding flowers for Hindu ceremonies, or white lilies and roses for Christian weddings, both well represented in the city's flower supply.

## Birthdays

Roses, orchids, and carnations suit personality-driven birthday gifting, representing the more universal, contemporary side of Kochi's genuinely diverse flower culture.
