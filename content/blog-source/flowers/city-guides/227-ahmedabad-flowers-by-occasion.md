# Best Flowers for Every Occasion in Ahmedabad

Ahmedabad's occasion calendar leans heavily on distinctly Gujarati festivals, and matching flower choice to each occasion's specific character matters more here than defaulting to generic national gifting conventions.

## Navratri

Bright, festive mixed flowers suit the energy of Gujarat's garba and dandiya celebrations, with demand extending across the festival's full nine-night run rather than a single peak day.

## Diwali and Bestu Varas

Warm-toned flowers, particularly marigold, suit both Diwali itself and the Gujarati New Year celebrated the following day — worth considering a slightly extended gifting window here compared to cities where Diwali gifting concentrates around one date.

## Janmashtami

Traditional flowers suited to devotional offerings, particularly marigold and tuberose, suit the occasion's significance within Gujarat's strong Vaishnav traditions.

## Uttarayan

Bright, cheerful mixed bouquets suit the festive, outdoor energy of kite-flying season, distinct from the more devotional tone of Ahmedabad's other major festivals.

## Weddings

Roses, tuberose, and chrysanthemums suit Ahmedabad's wedding gifting, following broader Gujarati wedding flower conventions that favor abundant, fragrant arrangements.

## Birthdays

Roses, gerberas, and mixed bouquets suit personality-driven birthday gifting, following similar logic to birthday gifting nationally.

**What flowers suit Navratri gifting in Ahmedabad?**

**Should Diwali gifting in Ahmedabad extend beyond the main festival day?**
Worth considering, given that Bestu Varas, the Gujarati New Year, is celebrated the day after Diwali, extending the region's festival gifting window.

**What flowers suit Janmashtami gifting in Ahmedabad?**
Marigold and tuberose suit the occasion's devotional character, reflecting Gujarat's strong Vaishnav traditions.
