# Best Flowers for Every Occasion in Chennai

Chennai's gifting calendar leans more heavily on religious and cultural occasions than many Indian cities, and matching flower variety to each occasion's traditional associations matters more here than defaulting to a generic bouquet.

## Pongal

Traditional flowers like marigold and jasmine suit Pongal's harvest-festival character well, particularly when paired with the broader festive, family-gathering tone the occasion carries across Tamil Nadu.

## Karthigai Deepam

Given the festival's association with light and devotion, warm-toned traditional flowers — marigold and mixed temple-appropriate varieties — suit the occasion better than a Western-style romantic bouquet.

## Navratri Golu

Flowers used in home displays and offerings during Golu celebrations tend toward traditional varieties like jasmine and marigold, reflecting the occasion's devotional, display-oriented character rather than a purely gift-focused one.

## Tamil New Year

Fresh, traditional flowers — mango leaves alongside jasmine and marigold in home decoration — mark the occasion's emphasis on new beginnings, with flower gifting to family often accompanying the broader celebration.

## Weddings

Jasmine holds particular significance in South Indian weddings specifically, often woven into the bride's hair and used extensively in wedding decor — a detail worth knowing if you're sending flowers related to a Chennai wedding rather than defaulting to a generic rose-based arrangement.

## Temple Occasions

Lotus, jasmine, and marigold suit temple-related gifting and offerings, given their established devotional associations in Tamil religious practice.

## Birthdays

Roses and mixed bouquets suit the more contemporary, personality-driven birthday gifting that runs alongside Chennai's traditional occasion calendar, following similar logic to birthday gifting nationally.
