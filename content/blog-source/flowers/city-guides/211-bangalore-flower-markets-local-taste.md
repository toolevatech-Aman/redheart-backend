# Bangalore's Flower Markets and What They Tell You About Local Taste

Bangalore's flower trade centers heavily on one historic wholesale hub, and what moves through it reflects a city balancing traditional South Indian flower use with a fast-growing, cosmopolitan gifting market.

## KR Market: The City's Central Flower Hub

Krishnarajendra Market, widely known as KR Market, functions as Bangalore's primary wholesale flower hub, supplying much of the city's retail florists and delivery services. Its role in the city's flower trade predates Bangalore's tech-hub identity by decades, making it one of the clearer threads connecting the city's older commercial character to its present-day gifting economy.

## What Moves Through in the Highest Volume

Roses, carnations, orchids, sunflowers, and mixed seasonal bouquets dominate Bangalore's flower trade — a mix that leans more toward contemporary, romantic, and corporate-appropriate gifting than the heavily festival-and-temple-oriented flower use common in some other South Indian cities. This reflects Bangalore's demographic shift toward a younger, more cosmopolitan population over the past few decades.

## A Market Serving Both Old and New Bangalore

KR Market continues to serve traditional buyers — households, temples, and festival-driven demand — while also increasingly supplying the volume needed for the city's corporate gifting and modern romantic occasions. This dual role gives Bangalore's flower supply more range than a market built around a single dominant use case.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Given how much of Bangalore's flower volume centers on roses, carnations, and orchids rather than more traditional temple-and-festival varieties, these tend to be the most reliably fresh and available options for delivery on short notice — useful to know if timing matters more than seeking out a specific niche variety.
