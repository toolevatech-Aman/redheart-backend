# Kochi's Flower Markets and What They Tell You About Local Taste

Kochi's flower trade centers on a well-established Ernakulam market, supplying a city whose flower demand spans genuinely distinct religious and cultural traditions.

## Broadway Market: Kochi's Central Flower Hub

Broadway Market in Ernakulam functions as Kochi's primary flower trading hub, supplying retail florists and delivery services across the city's spread-out geography, from Fort Kochi and Mattancherry on the historic peninsula to Kakkanad and Aluva further inland.

## What Moves Through in the Highest Volume

Marigolds, chrysanthemums, roses, orchids, lotus, and carnations dominate Kochi's flower trade — a genuinely broad mix reflecting the city's dual religious calendar, with marigold and lotus suited to Onam and Vishu, and roses and carnations covering the substantial Christmas and Easter gifting the city's Christian population drives.

## A Market Serving Kerala's Backwater Geography

Kochi's unusual geography — a mix of islands, peninsulas, and mainland areas connected by bridges and waterways — means flower distribution from a central hub like Broadway Market has to cover a more spread-out delivery footprint than a more geographically compact city might require.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Given the breadth of Kochi's flower demand across Onam, Vishu, Christmas, and Easter, the city's markets maintain a genuinely wide variety in consistent supply — marigold and lotus for Hindu festival occasions, roses and carnations for Christian ones, both reliably available depending on the specific occasion.
