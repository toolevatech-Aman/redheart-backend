# Why Flower Gifting Means So Much in Lonavala

Lonavala isn't a typical residential city — most flower orders here go to a resort room, a weekend cottage, or a couple mid-getaway, shaping a gifting culture built around surprise rather than routine occasions.

Given how transient Lonavala's population is — mostly weekend visitors from Mumbai and Pune rather than long-term residents — flower gifting centers on marking a specific trip or celebration rather than the personal and festival occasions that shape gifting in a typical city.
