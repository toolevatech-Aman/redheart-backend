# Ahmedabad's Flower Markets and What They Tell You About Local Taste

Ahmedabad's flower trade centers on a historic market that supplies both the city's dense festival calendar and its everyday gifting demand.

## Jamalpur Market: Ahmedabad's Flower Trading Hub

Jamalpur Market functions as the city's primary flower wholesale hub, supplying retail florists and delivery services across Ahmedabad and extending to Gandhinagar. Its role has grown alongside the city's expansion into newer commercial and residential areas.

## What Moves Through in the Highest Volume

Marigolds, roses, tuberose, chrysanthemums, gerberas, and mixed seasonal flowers dominate Ahmedabad's flower trade — a mix weighted toward traditional festival and devotional use (marigold especially) alongside a growing contemporary romantic and celebratory segment.

## Marigold's Central Role

Given how heavily Ahmedabad's occasion calendar leans on festivals like Navratri, Diwali, and Janmashtami, marigold's prominence in local flower volume reflects genuine sustained demand rather than seasonal spikes alone — it functions almost as a baseline flower in the city's gifting and devotional culture.

## What This Means as a Buyer

Marigold, roses, and tuberose tend to be the most consistently fresh and available choices for delivery in Ahmedabad, given how much volume moves through Jamalpur Market in these varieties specifically.
