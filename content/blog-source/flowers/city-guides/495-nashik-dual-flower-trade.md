# Nashik's Dual Flower Trade: Pilgrimage and Modern Growth

Nashik's identity as a Kumbh Mela site sits alongside its modern reputation as India's wine capital, giving the city's flower trade a genuinely dual character.

## Pilgrimage-Driven Demand
Given Nashik's status as home to the Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga, marigold and traditional devotional flowers move through local trade in steady volume tied to ongoing pilgrimage, independent of specific festival peaks.

## A Modern, Cosmopolitan Layer
Nashik's emergence as India's wine capital has brought a more cosmopolitan dimension to the city, supporting demand for roses and contemporary arrangements alongside its traditional devotional flower use.

Marigold, given its central role in the city's ongoing pilgrimage and temple-related demand.
