# Why Flower Gifting Means So Much in Patna

Patna's identity as ancient Pataliputra, once the capital of the Maurya Empire, gives the city one of the deepest historical foundations of any major Indian city — a heritage that sits alongside Bihar's most significant modern festival as the defining occasion in the city's flower-gifting calendar.

## Chhath Puja: Bihar's Greatest Festival

Chhath Puja stands as Bihar's most significant festival, and Patna, as the state capital along the Ganga riverfront, hosts some of the most substantial Chhath celebrations anywhere. Flower demand tied to this festival specifically reaches a scale that dwarfs most other occasions in the city's calendar, given how central Chhath is to Bihari cultural identity.

## A City Along a Sacred and Historic River

Patna's location on the Ganga connects the city to the same riverfront ritual traditions found in other Ganga cities like Varanasi, while its Mauryan-era history as Pataliputra adds a distinct layer of ancient significance that predates most other major Indian cities' historical identities by centuries.

## Diwali Within Bihar's Broader Calendar

Diwali remains a major occasion in Patna, observed alongside Chhath Puja as part of a broader festival season that runs from Diwali through the Chhath celebrations that typically follow shortly after.

## Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti's Local Significance

Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti carries particular significance in Patna specifically, tied to the city's historical connection to the Sikh Guru's birth, giving this occasion more local weight here than in many other Indian cities.

## What This Means for Choosing a Gift

Given how dominant Chhath Puja is in Patna's flower-gifting calendar, timing and flower choice for this specific festival matter more here than almost any other single occasion in the city's yearly cycle — traditional flowers suited to riverfront ritual offerings are worth prioritizing during this period specifically.
