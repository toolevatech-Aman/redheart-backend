# Best Flowers for Every Occasion in Udaipur

Udaipur's occasion calendar blends Rajasthan's traditional festivals with the city's own celebrated identity as a premier romantic and destination-wedding location.

Roses, orchids, and lilies suit Udaipur's signature romantic occasion well, with the city's elegant reputation supporting more elaborate, premium arrangements than routine anniversary gifting elsewhere.

## Destination Weddings

Roses, marigolds, and tuberose suit Udaipur's substantial wedding trade, with the city's palace and lake settings often calling for arrangements that match the elevated, elegant tone of the location itself.

## Mewar Festival

Traditional, festive flowers suit gifting tied to this spring cultural celebration of the region's Mewar heritage.

## Teej

Bright, festive flowers suit Teej's themes of marital happiness and monsoon celebration, consistent with the festival's significance across Rajasthan.

## Gangaur

Traditional devotional flowers, particularly marigold, suit Gangaur's significance within Rajasthan's festival calendar.
