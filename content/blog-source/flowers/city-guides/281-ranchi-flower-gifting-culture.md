# Why Flower Gifting Means So Much in Ranchi

Ranchi's identity as Jharkhand's capital, shaped significantly by the region's Adivasi cultural heritage, gives the city an occasion calendar genuinely distinct from most other major Indian cities.

## Sarhul: A Festival Rooted in Adivasi Tradition

Sarhul, the Adivasi spring festival, holds significant cultural weight in Ranchi, celebrating the region's indigenous traditions in a way that gives the city's flower-gifting calendar a character found in relatively few other major urban centers in India.

## Palash: A Flower Tied to Regional Identity

Palash, alongside more conventional roses and marigolds, holds a place in Ranchi's flower culture connected to the region's specific botanical and cultural identity — a flower less commonly featured in gifting content from other Indian cities, reflecting Jharkhand's distinct regional character.

## Karma Puja's Local Significance

Karma Puja, another occasion connected to the region's Adivasi and broader Jharkhand traditions, adds further cultural depth to Ranchi's calendar, celebrated with significance distinct from the state's more universally observed festivals.

## Chhath Puja and Diwali Within the Broader Calendar

Alongside its distinctive regional occasions, Ranchi observes Chhath Puja and Diwali, connecting the city to the broader Eastern Indian festival calendar shared with neighboring Bihar and West Bengal.

## Government Ceremonies as a Notable Occasion Category

As the capital of Jharkhand, Ranchi's status as a seat of state government generates a distinct category of official and ceremonial gifting tied to government functions and civic occasions, less pronounced in cities without this administrative role.

## What This Means for Choosing a Gift

Given Ranchi's genuinely distinctive Adivasi cultural calendar, particularly Sarhul and the use of Palash, choosing flowers that acknowledge this regional specificity — rather than assuming a generic North or East Indian festival calendar applies uniformly — reflects real thoughtfulness for gifting tied to these occasions.
